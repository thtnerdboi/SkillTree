# Play Store Listing

## App Name
ArcStep's SkillTree

## Short Description
Level up daily habits with quests, XP, streaks, and a skill tree.

## Full Description
ArcStep's SkillTree turns self-improvement into a focused RPG-style progression system. Instead of staring at a blank habit tracker, you build a personal skill tree through small, practical challenges that help you improve your mind, body, and craft.

Start by setting your goals. SkillTree helps shape them into daily quests across three growth pillars:

Mind: build clarity, focus, reflection, and creativity.
Body: strengthen energy, recovery, mobility, nutrition, and endurance.
Craft: practice learning, output, discipline, expression, and mastery.

Complete challenges to earn XP, keep your streak alive, unlock new nodes, and see your progress grow level by level. Every action is designed to feel achievable today while still moving you toward the person you want to become.

What you can do with SkillTree:

- Complete bite-size daily challenges
- Earn XP and level up through a visual skill tree
- Unlock new skill nodes as your consistency improves
- Track progress across Mind, Body, and Craft
- Build streaks with daily reminders
- Use AI-powered challenge generation to personalize your journey
- Connect with friends and compare progress
- Keep climbing with prestige-style long-term progression

SkillTree is built for people who want structure without pressure. Use it for fitness, study, creative practice, mindfulness, career growth, or any personal goal that becomes easier when it is broken into daily quests.

SkillTree Pro is optional and adds premium features for users who want a more personalized experience, including faster XP progression, an ad-free app experience, and priority AI challenge generation.

Your growth does not need to feel like a spreadsheet. Build your tree, finish your quests, and level up your life one challenge at a time.

## Content Rating Justification
ArcStep's SkillTree contains no violence, no adult content, no sexual content, no gambling, and no user-generated explicit content. The app focuses on personal development, habit tracking, daily challenges, and progress visualization.
